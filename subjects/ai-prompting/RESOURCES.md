# AI Prompting Resources

## Knowledge

- [Paper: _The Prompt Report: A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering Techniques_ by Schulhoff et al. (arXiv:2406.06608)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608)
  Local copy: `sources/the-prompt-report.pdf` (CC BY 4.0). Systematic survey that builds a taxonomy of text-based prompting techniques, a shared vocabulary of prompting terms, and covers multilingual/multimodal prompting, agents, evaluation and security. Use for: the canonical vocabulary (seed for `GLOSSARY.md`), technique definitions, and the citation behind any claim about a technique.
- [Site: The Prompt Report companion site](https://trigaten.github.io/Prompt_Survey_Site/)
  The authors' landing page for the survey. Use for: links to the latest version and the authors' supporting material.
